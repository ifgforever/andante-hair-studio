Services

Hair salon services in Portage Park, Chicago

Andante is a full-service hair studio on North Central Avenue, cutting women's, men's and children's hair. Below is what happens in the chair. For what any of it costs, or how long it takes, call the shop — it depends on your hair.

Call about your service

Women's haircuts & styling

Cuts, trims, layers, bangs, blowouts and styling. We start with your hair's actual texture and how much time you are willing to spend on it in the morning, then cut to that.

Men's haircuts

Scissor cuts, clipper cuts, tapers and fades. Straightforward, quick, and cut the same way the next time you come in.

Color & highlights

Single-process color, gray coverage, highlights and lowlights. Color takes longer than a cut and the result depends on where your hair is starting from, so please call ahead rather than walking in for it.

Beard & neckline

Beard trims, shaping and neckline cleanup. Easy to add onto a cut — mention it when you sit down.

Kids' haircuts

Kids are welcome at Andante. If it is a first haircut, say so when you call and we will make sure there is time for it to go slowly.

Not sure which of these you need, or want to know what something costs? Call (773) 283-2770 during shop hours and just ask. Nobody is going to upsell you over the phone.

Good to know

Before you come in

Call first. One phone call saves a trip. Color especially should be booked ahead — it holds a chair a lot longer than a cut does.

Hours. Tuesday through Saturday, 10am to 6pm. Closed Sunday and Monday. Every service has to finish by close, so call before heading over late in the afternoon.

Payment. Andante is a cash shop — please plan on cash rather than a card.

Tuesday through Saturday, 10am to 6pm. We will tell you what is open today.
