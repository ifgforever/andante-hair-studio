What you are getting

Curl and body that stay put

A perm reshapes the hair itself rather than sitting on top of it. That is the whole appeal: you wash it, you sleep on it, and the shape is still there in the morning. No round brush, no wand, no thirty minutes in front of a mirror before work.

Two things people usually mean when they ask for one:

A classic perm — defined, lasting curl. Best when you want a real change in texture and are willing to learn how to dry it properly.

A body wave — a looser, softer version. It does not read as curly so much as thick. This is the one for fine or flat hair that will not hold a blowout past noon, and it is what most people actually want when they say they wish their hair "did something."

Which one suits you depends on your hair's texture, length and condition, and on how much effort you want to put in afterward. That is a conversation, not a menu selection, which is why it starts with a phone call.

Before you book

Worth knowing first

It takes a couple of hours

Hair gets wrapped rod by rod, processed, neutralized and rinsed. How long depends on how much hair there is and how tight a curl you want. This is not a lunch-break service, and the chair gets booked accordingly — another reason Andante works by appointment rather than walk-in.

It grows out, it does not wash out

"Permanent" means the curl stays until the hair grows. Most people get several months before the straight new growth at the root makes it worth revisiting. A soft body wave will relax sooner than a tight curl.

Color changes the answer

Perming and lightening both stress the hair, and stacking them too closely is how people end up with damage they did not sign up for. If your hair has been colored, highlighted, bleached or relaxed, say so on the phone and say when. You will get a straight answer about whether to go ahead, wait a few weeks, or leave it alone — including if the answer is no.

Afterward

Leave it alone for the first couple of days: no washing, no tying it back, no clips. After that it is ordinary hair care, with a gentler hand than you are probably using now.

Not sure a perm is the right call for your hair? Say so when you ring. Talking someone out of one is a normal part of the job.
