What we do

Cuts, color and styling — for the whole family

Andante is a full-service hair studio, which means women's, men's and children's hair all get cut in the same room. A household does not have to split up to get haircuts.

Haircuts & styling

Cuts, trims, blowouts and styling for women and men, worked around the hair you actually have rather than a picture on a wall.

Color

Color and highlighting services. Call ahead for color — it books longer than a cut and we like to talk it through first.

Men's haircuts

Men's cuts, tapers, fades and clipper work, plus beard and neckline cleanup. In and out on your lunch break.
