What we do

Everyone in the family, one room.

Andante is a full-service hair studio, which means women's, men's and children's hair all get cut in the same place. Nobody has to make two stops on a Saturday.

Cuts and styling, color and highlights, men's cuts and beard cleanup, and kids — including the nervous first-timers. Everything here is by appointment, so one call gets you a time that is actually yours.

In the chair

Cuts, color and everything between

Haircuts & styling

Cuts, trims, layers, bangs and blowouts, worked around the hair you actually have and the time you actually have in the morning.

Color & highlights

Single-process color, gray coverage, highlights and lowlights. Mention it when you book so the right amount of time is set aside.

Men's & kids'

Men's cuts, tapers, fades and beard cleanup. Kids are welcome, and a first haircut gets as long as it needs.
